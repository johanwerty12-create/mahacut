YOUR TWO-WEEK FOUNDATION

0 of 14 days practiced

Start with a clean setup. Come back tomorrow and pick up where you left off.

Use the plan flexibly. A day is a suggested practice session, not a deadline. Keep tools controlled and rehearse new cutting movements on a mannequin before working with a person.

DAYS 01–07

Set up your foundations

Learn the tools, head landmarks, and controlled movements before refining a blend.

DAYS 08–14

Build consistency

Rehearse the transition, compare your work, and repeat what needs more practice.

Practice safely. Start with tools off when learning a grip or path. Use training hair for cutting practice, protect the ears and neck, and get experienced guidance before skin-close work.
