    A SIMPLE WAY TO READ THE HEAD

    Beginner cutting map

    Use these zones to understand length change, not as a one-size-fits-all cutting formula.

    1. 1 · ShortestWhere the cut is closest.
    2. 2 · BlendThe gradual transition.
    3. 3 · LongerHair above the blend.
    4. 4 · TopLength set for the chosen style.

    Think: short → transition → longer. The chosen style and the head shape decide where each zone sits.
